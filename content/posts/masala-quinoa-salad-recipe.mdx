## Why Quinoa is a Superfood 🌾

Quinoa is one of the few plant foods that contains **all 9 essential amino acids**, making it a complete protein source — perfect for vegetarians and anyone wanting to build muscle or lose weight.

Unlike white rice, quinoa:
- Has **2x more protein**
- Is rich in **fiber** (keeps you full longer)
- Has a **low glycemic index** (no sugar spikes)
- Is packed with **iron, magnesium, and B vitamins**

---

## Ingredients (Serves 2)

**For the salad:**
- 1 cup quinoa (rinsed)
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 cucumber, diced
- ½ red onion, finely chopped
- 1 green chilli, finely chopped (optional)
- Large handful fresh coriander, chopped
- ¼ cup roasted peanuts or chickpeas

**For the masala dressing:**
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- ½ tsp cumin powder
- ½ tsp chaat masala
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- Salt to taste

---

## Method

1. **Cook quinoa:** Add quinoa and 2 cups water to a pot. Bring to a boil, reduce heat, cover and simmer for 12–15 minutes until water is absorbed. Fluff with a fork and let cool slightly.

2. **Make the dressing:** Whisk together lemon juice, olive oil, cumin powder, chaat masala, black pepper, and salt in a small bowl.

3. **Assemble:** In a large bowl, combine cooked quinoa, tomatoes, cucumber, red onion, chilli, and coriander.

4. **Dress and toss:** Pour the masala dressing over the salad and toss well to combine.

5. **Top and serve:** Top with roasted peanuts or chickpeas. Serve immediately or refrigerate for up to 2 days.

<InfoNote>Let the quinoa cool a little before adding vegetables so they stay crisp and fresh.</InfoNote>

## Variations

- 🥑 **Add avocado** for healthy fats and creaminess
- 🧀 **Add paneer cubes** for extra protein
- 🌿 **Add mint leaves** for a refreshing twist
- 🥗 **Add spinach** for extra iron and vitamins

---

## Meal Prep Tip 🍱

Make a big batch of quinoa on Sunday and store it in the fridge. Assemble fresh salads throughout the week — it takes only **5 minutes** when the quinoa is pre-cooked!

> 💡 *Pro Tip:* Add the dressing only just before eating to keep the salad crisp.
