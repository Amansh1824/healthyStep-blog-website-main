## What is HIIT and Why Does It Work?

**High-Intensity Interval Training (HIIT)** alternates between short bursts of intense effort and brief rest periods. In just 20 minutes, you can:

- 🔥 Burn up to **300–400 calories**
- ⚡ Boost your metabolism for hours **after** the workout (afterburn effect)
- 💪 Build lean muscle without any equipment
- ⏱️ Save time compared to long, slow cardio sessions

> 💡 *Science says:* HIIT can be more effective than 45 minutes of moderate jogging — in a fraction of the time!

---

## Before You Start

<InfoNote>Always warm up for 3–5 minutes. March in place, swing your arms, do slow leg circles. Stop if you feel sharp pain — discomfort is normal, pain is not.</InfoNote>

---

## The Workout — 4 Rounds

**Format:** 40 seconds work → 20 seconds rest

<WorkoutStep title="Jump Squats" time="40s" rest="20s" tip="Land softly with knees bent. Modify by doing regular squats if needed." />
<WorkoutStep title="Push-Ups" time="40s" rest="20s" tip="Keep core tight. Do incline push-ups (on a table) to make it easier." />
<WorkoutStep title="High Knees" time="40s" rest="20s" tip="Drive your knees to hip height. Pump your arms for extra calorie burn." />
<WorkoutStep title="Mountain Climbers" time="40s" rest="20s" tip="Keep hips level. Go slower if needed — control beats speed." />
<WorkoutStep title="Burpees" time="40s" rest="20s" tip="Step your feet back instead of jumping if you're a beginner." />

> **Rest 90 seconds between rounds.** Complete 4 rounds for the full 20-minute session.

---

## Cool Down (5 Minutes)

After the workout, spend 5 minutes cooling down:
- Child's pose — 60 seconds
- Standing quad stretch — 30 seconds each leg
- Seated hamstring stretch — 60 seconds
- Deep breathing — 60 seconds

## Progression Tips

- **Week 1–2:** Do 2 rounds only
- **Week 3–4:** Add a 3rd round
- **Week 5+:** Go for all 4 rounds

Consistency beats intensity every time. Start where you are, not where you want to be. 🌟
